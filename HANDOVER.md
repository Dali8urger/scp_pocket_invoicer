# SCP Pocket EA — your guide

Mate, this is a phone app that helps you with the paperwork side of jobs. Danielle set it up. Three minutes to learn.

## Install it once

1. Open the link Danielle sent in **Safari** (iPhone) or **Chrome** (Android).
2. Sign in with **shanecollinsplumbing@gmail.com**.
3. You'll get an email straight away. Open it on the same phone, tap **Sign in**.
4. Once you're in, add it to your home screen:
   - iPhone: Tap the share button (square with arrow), scroll down, **Add to Home Screen**, **Add**.
   - Android: Three-dot menu, **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.
5. You now have an SCP icon. Tap it like any other app.

The first time you record a voice note, the phone will ask for **microphone permission**. Tap **Allow**.

## The whole workflow, one job at a time

### When you arrive at a job
1. Open SCP.
2. Tap **+ Start a job**.
3. Customer name. Address. Optional one-liner about what they reckon is wrong.
4. Tap **Start job**.

That's it. The app's running in the background. Phone in pocket. Do the job.

### Add a photo any time
On the job screen, tap **📷 Add photo**. Phone camera opens. Snap. Done. Use it for water damage, the back of a hot water unit, a meter reading, whatever Danielle would want for the invoice.

### When you leave the job
1. Open SCP again. The job should still be there at the top, marked "In progress".
2. Tap into it.
3. **Park the ute first.** Then tap **🎙 Tap to start recording**.
4. Talk for up to 90 seconds. You're not writing an essay. Say:
   - What you did, in your own words
   - Any materials you used (e.g. "two tap washers, a tempering valve, half a metre of copper")
   - How long you were on site (e.g. "about an hour and a half")
   - Whether there's anything Danielle should chase up with the customer
5. Tap **⏹ Stop and transcribe**. Wait 10 seconds.
6. The transcript appears on screen. **Read it.** Fix any obvious mistakes (it's pretty good but not perfect on names and addresses).
7. Tap **Generate draft invoice**. Wait 15 seconds.
8. A draft invoice appears with line items. You don't have to make it perfect, Danielle will review every one. But if a line is obviously wrong, you can edit or delete it right there.
9. Tap **Save draft and review** to lock it in.
10. On the next screen, tap **Send draft to admin**.

Done. Danielle gets the draft in her email and handles Xero.

## What if you cocked up the recording?

No drama. From the transcript screen:
- Tap **Skip invoice, just save the note** — saves your voice note, no invoice generated. Danielle can build it later.
- Or hit **Back to edit transcript** if you got there from the preview.

## What if you forgot to start the job?

Also no drama. Just start it when you remember, even if you've already left. You can fudge the customer name and address. The voice note still works.

## What you'll see on the daily summary

Every day at 6pm Danielle gets a one-page email summarising your day: jobs done, draft invoices, total invoiced. So if you missed something the night before, she'll see the gap and ask.

## Tips

- **Park before recording.** Distracted driving is dodgy and the transcript will be rubbish anyway from road noise.
- **Speak naturally.** Don't try to sound like an invoice. "I was there about an hour, swapped two tap washers in the kitchen, charged a callout" works fine.
- **Mention quantities.** "Used three metres of copper" beats "used some copper".
- **Use customer names not pronouns.** "Jane wanted a quote on..." beats "she wanted a quote on..."
- **If you mention something Danielle should follow up on**, say "Danielle, follow up on..." in the recording. She reads every transcript.
